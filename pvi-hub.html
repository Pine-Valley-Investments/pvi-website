Branded SEO - Advisor Name Searches

Each advisor's bio page serves as their entity home - the canonical page Google associates with that person. For branded searches like "Jason Braatz financial advisor" or "Nick Lampone Pine Valley Investments", these are the levers that move rankings.

Title Tag Format (Live)

[Full Name] | [Job Title] | Pine Valley Investments → Jason Braatz | President, BWA | Pine Valley Investments → Harry Morad | CEO & Co-Founder | Pine Valley Investments

Meta Description Format (Live)

Meet [Name], [Title] at Pine Valley Investments. Serving South Jersey and the Philadelphia area from Marlton, NJ. Schedule a consultation today.

Person Schema - Key Fields (Live on All 40 Bios)

  • @id - Unique identifier linking the person entity back to their bio page URL. Creates a stable Knowledge Graph node.
  • sameAs - The critical connector. Links LinkedIn + FINRA BrokerCheck + SEC IAPD to the same entity. Google stops treating these as separate competing results.
  • worksFor @id - References the firm's FinancialService @id, creating a graph connection between advisor and firm.
  • hasCredential - Formally marks CFP®, CFA, ChFC designations with the recognizing organization (Chris O'Neill's CFA is live).
  • knowsAbout - Keyword array tailored by role (e.g., "Retirement Planning", "Portfolio Management", "Regulatory Compliance").
  • BreadcrumbList - Pine Valley Investments → Meet Your Team → Advisor Name. Powers the breadcrumb trail in Google results.

YMYL

The live vercel.json prevents the staging domain from getting indexed and prepares automatic redirection for when DNS switches.

GEO - Generative Engine Optimization

GEO optimizes content to appear in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude. AI systems use Retrieval-Augmented Generation: they search a web index, retrieve top results, read them, and synthesize an answer citing the most relevant sources.

STAT
Pages ranked 5th in traditional SERPs gained 115% visibility improvement from GEO tactics. 80% of sources cited by AI platforms don't appear in Google's top 100. This is a significant asymmetric opportunity for a firm PVI's size. (Princeton University, 2024)

Content Signals That Get Cited by AI

  • Statistics +41% visibility - Include concrete numbers every ~80 words: "$2.65B AUM", "40+ credentialed advisors", "serving 500+ families since 2015"
  • Answer capsules - Each H2 followed by a 40–60-word self-contained answer. ChatGPT draws 44% of citations from the first 30% of a page. Front-load the answer.
  • Expert citations +30–40% - Reference CFP Board data, Vanguard research, SEC guidelines with attribution
  • Avoid promotional language −26% - Educate first, sell second. Service pages should answer questions, not pitch
  • Content freshness ×3.2 - Pages updated within 30 days receive 3.2x more Perplexity citations. Refresh key pages quarterly.
