September 15, 2026

Midterms, Oil
& a Whole Lot of Noise

10-year hits 5.00%. Buyback blackout begins Sep 15 — 40% of S&P enters quiet period. Discretionary positioning at widest gap vs. market-weight since bull market began. Hedge funds bought US TMT in 10 of 11 sessions at 97th-percentile pace. Goldman: S&P averaged +9% in 12 months after the first hike. Software revenue per employee gone parabolic.

Nicholas Lampone
Chief Strategy Officer & Investment Advisor · Pine Valley Investments

U.S. Futures & World Markets

Stocks are flat premarket as the market digests 10-year US Treasury yields hitting new post-2020 highs. Lingering concerns on AI development and crude sitting around $100 per barrel have investors hitting the pause button.

On midterms: Polymarket as of Friday put Republicans retaining the House at just 12%. A Democratic sweep has even odds. But changing your stock market exposure based on the likely election outcome is probably a bad idea. The average stock market performance for all six-month periods following midterm elections since 1970 has been good. During Obama's two terms, healthcare had a 14.2% annualized return — despite him campaigning on overhauling it. Despite Biden's green credentials, fossil-fuel companies were the best performers when he was in office. Earnings and interest rates still matter a lot more than Washington headlines.

S&P Futures vs. Fair Value: flat  |  10-Year Yield: 5.00%

Charts & Data

Aggregate equity positioning at the 58th percentile. Discretionary investors at the 34th percentile (modestly underweight); systematic strategies at the 86th percentile — highest in 11 months. Deutsche Bank via Daily Chartbook.

CTAs sold $16B of global equities last week. At current price and volatility, CTAs are expected to sell another $19.5B this week. Goldman via The Market Ear.

Asset managers and institutional investors remain clearly underexposed to equities. The gap vs. market-weight positioning is the widest since this bull market began in late 2022. Duality Research via Daily Chartbook.

Hedge funds net bought US TMT stocks in 10 of the last 11 sessions — the recent pace of long buying ranks in the 97th percentile on a 5-year lookback. Goldman via Daily Chartbook.

Buyback blackout begins this week on Sep 15 — approximately 40% of S&P 500 corporates enter their quiet periods, tempering one of the market's most consistent demand sources. Goldman via The Market Ear.

SPX 1-month put skew jumped to the 73rd percentile from the 36th percentile just a week ago — hedging demand picked up sharply. Mandy Xu, CBOE via Daily Chartbook.

For the first time since early April 2026, more NYSE stocks are making 52-week lows than 52-week highs. Grant Hawkridge via Daily Chartbook.

S&P 500 has generated an average return of +9% during the 12 months following the first hike. Goldman expects the bull market to continue. Goldman via Daily Chartbook.

S&P 500 forward earnings rising at a faster pace in recent weeks — the underlying trend in actual earnings remains very strongly to the upside. Yardeni Research via Daily Chartbook.
