$81 Billion and Counting,
Patience Pays

Anthropic's $81 billion IPO priced at $83, above the $70-$78 range, and the offering was upsized; S&P 500 futures are up over 1% premarket as the index looks to snap a 5-day losing streak. The VIX closed yesterday at 24.60 against a long-run average of 19.50. Nomura: aggregate SPX dealer gamma has plunged to negative $7.3 billion, the deepest reading since January 2019.

Nicholas Lampone
Chief Strategy Officer & Investment Advisor · Pine Valley Investments

U.S. Futures & World Markets

Nothing like the biggest IPO in history to get the market's juices flowing. Anthropic's $81 billion IPO was priced above the expected range at $83 per share (versus the $70-$78 range), and the offering was upsized. Clearly, the appetite for the AI trade is still there, even after the recent beatdown in technology stocks.

S&P 500 equity futures are indicated up over 1% premarket as the index looks to snap its 5-day losing streak. More importantly, the VIX is down 10% this morning. The VIX has remained stubbornly higher than its long-run average (19.50), closing yesterday at 24.60. To confirm any rally, we'll need to see if it moves lower in the next week or two.

With all of the recent volatility and talk about NVDA, SPCX, and the Anthropic IPO, I thought I'd share this old quote from Warren Buffett about trading and timing the market: "The stock market is designed to transfer money from the active to the patient."

We certainly want to be opportunistic when the market presents opportunities, but there's a big difference between taking advantage of volatility and getting caught up in it. The headlines change every day. Your long-term investment goals shouldn't.

Charts & Data

The Atlanta Fed's GDPNow model estimate for Q4 GDP growth declined to 3.2% from 3.4%. Augur Infinity via Daily Chartbook (Q4 GDPNow).

Looking into the bond market to find a 'crisis' in spreads, there is none to be found. Spreads by HY technology bonds have widened, but they've been wider. Spreads for consumer staples have, in contrast, collapsed. Nothing like a little inflation to boost expectations for the ability to service debt. Steven Blitz, TS Lombard via Daily Chartbook (credit spreads).

Discretionary equity positioning has generally moved inversely to rate volatility in recent years. The current decline is sharper than the MOVE index would suggest. Deutsche Bank via @isabelnet_sa and Daily Chartbook (equity positioning and interest rate volatility).

Global hedge funds have dumped Tech at an unprecedented pace over the last 6 weeks. Goldman Sachs via @wallstjesus and Daily Chartbook (hedge funds and technology stocks).

Value exposure has surged to five-year highs amid growing stagflation concerns. Goldman Sachs via Zero Hedge and Daily Chartbook (hedge funds and value stocks).

Retail net bought $6.19 billion of US equities last week, the largest weekly total of the 11 weeks since September 1 ... The buying was concentrated in semis and mega-cap tech ... while no single name saw more than $49 million of net selling. Goldman Sachs via Zero Hedge and Daily Chartbook (retail trading activity).

Aggregate SPX dealer gamma has plunged to negative $7.3 billion, by a wide margin the deepest reading on Nomura's chart going back to January 2019 ... dealers are now selling into every dip and buying into every rip. (If you're wondering why stocks have been so volatile, the options market is intensifying every move, in both directions.) Charlie McElligott, Nomura via Zero Hedge and Daily Chartbook (dealer gamma).

The VIX Index has moved up above the prices of all of its futures contracts ... It is a fairly rare condition, and associated with a bottoming condition for prices whenever it happens. But caveat time: A bottoming condition is not necessarily the same as the exact moment of the bottom. Tom McClellan via Luke Kawa, Sherwood and Daily Chartbook (spot VIX vs. VIX futures).

Comparing a selection of traditional factors with their relative opposites, Pure Value versus Pure Growth shows the largest YTD outperformance of the four pairings. It is not alone, though: Momentum, Quality and Low Volatility are also ahead so far this year, albeit more modestly. S&P Global via Daily Chartbook (factor investing performance).

This is arguably the most important chart for stock bulls. It's the ratio of growth vs. value (VUG/VTV). The last two major market bottoms in April 2025 & March 2026 occurred once VUG/VTV produced a bullish RSI divergence. We're forming another one right now, at the same level. @calebfranzen via Daily Chartbook (what are growth stocks and value stocks telling us?).

Tech is extremely oversold relative to Health Care by historical standards, and we think it will outperform over the next 100 trading days. Jessica Rabe, DataTrek Research via Daily Chartbook (tech vs. healthcare stocks).

The relative performance of MCG & Tech has dipped slightly below its long-run trend. Since this level has triggered five sharp rebounds since 2024, the odds still favor an overweight position. Deutsche Bank via Daily Chartbook (mega cap growth & tech).
