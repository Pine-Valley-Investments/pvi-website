Nvidia Delivers.
Now What?

Per Briefing.com, Nvidia raised its FY28 revenue growth forecast to 75% year-over-year, yet NVDA is only +2% premarket. Attention turns to the Anthropic IPO, reportedly heavily oversubscribed. BofA: investors cut their tech overweight by the most since 2006, and 52% now expect a hyperscaler to announce a capex cut within six months.

Nicholas Lampone
Chief Strategy Officer & Investment Advisor · Pine Valley Investments

U.S. Futures & World Markets

Nvidia's blowout earnings are helping support stocks and the AI trade this morning. Per Briefing.com: The semiconductor giant raised its FY28 revenue growth forecast to 75% year-over-year, reinforcing confidence in AI infrastructure demand despite recent concerns about the sustainability of spending.

NVDA is +2% premarket, which feels like an underwhelming reaction considering the size of the beat and the outlook. This indicates there is still a massive overhang the stock must get through before it can meaningfully rally. Even blowing the doors off earnings isn't enough to get everyone off the sidelines.

We got through one big event this week, but now all attention turns to the Anthropic IPO. Reports indicate ANT is heavily oversubscribed, meaning it may price on the high end of the range (initial range was $70-$78), or even issue more shares.

Nvidia has delivered the goods, and now the market needs to decide what they're worth. The Anthropic IPO will provide another interesting read on investor appetite for the AI trade. One thing is certain: there's no shortage of excitement surrounding this space.

Keep your seatbelt fastened!
