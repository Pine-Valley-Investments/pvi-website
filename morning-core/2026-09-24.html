Bonds Are Having
a Moment

10-year at 5.14%. NY Fed Williams sees no urgency for October hike. Flash PMI: fastest output growth in over 5 years — but input cost inflation hit highest since Oct 2022. National diesel prices at $6.52/gal. AI compute costs falling 47%/quarter since 2023 — 4x faster than DNA sequencing. Equal-weight S&P down 5% from Aug peak.

Chief Strategy Officer & Investment Advisor · Pine Valley Investments

U.S. Futures & World Markets

Stocks are set to open lower as higher interest rates put pressure on equities. The big question is whether stock volatility plays catch-up higher or if interest rates slide lower.

Leon Cooperman's famous quote from the late '90s still rings true: "You don't want to live in a world where the Fed cannot get whatever result it wants."

Charts & Data

US business output growing at the fastest rate in over five years in September. Chris Williamson, S&P Global via Daily Chartbook.

But input cost inflation surged — hitting the highest since October 2022. S&P Global via Daily Chartbook: the growth/inflation tension is intensifying.

National diesel prices at $6.52/gal — US preparing a potential 90-day diesel export ban. @kevrgordon via Daily Chartbook.

Diesel is embedded in the delivered cost of nearly every physical good. The rise in diesel prices migrates into core goods and services with a lag — exactly the kind of pass-through the Fed cannot dismiss as transitory. Torsten Slok, Apollo.

Investor allocations to bonds have drifted to the lowest level since immediately prior to the Global Financial Crisis. @callum_thomas via Daily Chartbook.

Discretionary investors still positioned cautiously relative to the strength of earnings growth — more cash could move into stocks. Deutsche Bank via Daily Chartbook.

AI compute costs falling ~47%/quarter since 2023 — 4x faster than DNA sequencing, 6x faster than compute, 18x faster than lithium batteries. Epoch AI via Daily Chartbook.

S&P 500 cumulative A/D line dropped more than 1,500 points from its peak while the index itself was down barely 2%. Prior periods with similar breadth deterioration saw little negative forward return impact. Bespoke via Daily Chartbook.
