November 5, 2026

Election Euphoria,
Can It Last?

Stocks flat after the post-election rally. Cyclicals rip while semis stay weak — a healthier, broader rally. Gas wholesale prices hit a 7-month low. Retail has been a net buyer for 22 straight sessions (~$33B). CTA equity positioning at -3.3 SD, the lowest since March 31. S&P semis at 13.8x forward earnings, a 25% discount to the S&P — the widest since December 2018.

Nicholas Lampone
Chief Strategy Officer & Investment Advisor · Pine Valley Investments

U.S. Futures & World Markets

Stocks are relatively flat premarket as investors digest yesterday's post-election rally. We're seeing some big upside moves on earnings results in APP, SEI, COHR, and TM, while other stocks (BROS, CAVA, EXPE, MELI, FICO, and SNAP) got smacked on weak results or guidance.

The bulls seized on the narrative that the election was a "clearing event," along with some favorable seasonality trends (see chart below). Hyperscalers like AMZN ripped higher on the narrative that Democrats will slow the pace of datacenter construction. It was interesting to see cyclical stocks rip while semis remained weak. Either way, seeing the rally broaden out to other sectors is a healthy sign. A little participation outside of Big Tech never hurts.

Coming up, we have initial claims data and Q3 nonfarm productivity data on the economic calendar today, plus a few Fed speakers dropping hints on interest rates. Tomorrow, we get the October employment report, and we know a strong labor market has supported this tape. Another data point for the bulls to keep an eye on.

Charts & Data

Even after adjusting for inflation, incomes are up almost 30% from the 2013 lows. It's hard to believe real incomes were unchanged from 1989 to 2013. Ritholtz Wealth (household income).

Median net worth has experienced a similar trajectory with a little more volatility. The fact that both housing and stock prices have boomed this decade has helped push median household net worth to new highs. Ritholtz Wealth (household net worth).

US wholesale gasoline prices plunge to a 7-month low below 275 cents per gallon. If the drop holds, American drivers are days away from enjoying the cheapest retail gasoline since March. @javierblas via Daily Chartbook.

$215M in Bitcoin shorts liquidated in just one hour. Short squeeze. @ki_young_ju via Daily Chartbook.

Retail has been a net buyer of US cash equities on our platform for 22 consecutive sessions since October 5, accumulating nearly $33B in the sixth-longest and fifth-largest buying streak since 2017. Scott Rubner, Citadel via Daily Chartbook.

The rotation once again toward Semiconductors has been particularly pronounced. Activity ran 15% above its trailing-year average, with the sector accounting for nearly 40% of single-stock premium, roughly double its share a year ago. Scott Rubner, Citadel via Daily Chartbook.

Small-cap positioning is still very light, sitting in the 22nd percentile. If sentiment shifts, there's room for a meaningful pickup in risk-taking. Deutsche Bank via @isabelnet_sa.

Our CTA model estimates aggregate US equity positioning at -3.3 standard deviations — in the sub-2nd percentile of its one-year range and the lowest since March 31. Positioning has fallen from +1.7σ three months ago to -1.0σ one month ago, with CTAs now short for 26 consecutive sessions. Scott Rubner, Citadel via Daily Chartbook.

Same stocks, different weights. The cap- and equal-weighted S&P 500 correlation has plummeted to 37%. Do you focus on the stock market or the market of stocks? @michaelkantro via Daily Chartbook.

As of October 28, when Energy and Tech were the top two best sector performers within the S&P 500, the correlation between them was negative — the first negative reading in Ned Davis Research's data going back to at least 1973. NDR via Charles Schwab.

Starting with the 82nd Congress in 1951, a Democratic Congress and Republican President is the most likely combo at 22 years (out of 75). But the two worst years ever (1974 and 2008) happened in these years, along with the 5th and 6th worst (1957 and 1973). Of course, all of those years were also recessions. Carson Research via @ryandetrick.

The S&P 500 semiconductors now trade at 13.8x forward earnings. Relative to the S&P 500, Semiconductors trade at a 25% discount, the widest since December 2018 — despite forward earnings estimates rising 30% since June. Scott Rubner, Citadel via Daily Chartbook.

Year three has been the best year of the presidential cycle. 2027 is year three. Sean Emory, Investing with Data via Daily Chartbook.
