November 17, 2026

AI Reality Check,
Bulletproof Apple

Stocks are mixed as tech tries to bounce after Nasdaq -2.5% and SOX -5.3%; the market awaits Nvidia earnings after the close. Three-month moves: CRWV -52%, SMCI -37%, VRT -35%, ORCL -20%, while Apple is +15% and within 2% of its all-time high. Goldman: US Info Tech hedge fund long/short ratio hit 1.5, the lowest on record.

Nicholas Lampone
Chief Strategy Officer & Investment Advisor · Pine Valley Investments

U.S. Futures & World Markets

Stocks are mixed this morning as tech stocks try to bounce after yesterday's ugly session (Nasdaq -2.5% and SOX -5.3%). AI and semiconductor names are showing a bit of strength on the Reuters headline below about blocking imports of Chinese data center equipment.

Any way you slice it, the entire market is waiting for Nvidia's earnings after the closing bell today. The market will also have to digest Anthropic's IPO on Thursday. No shortage of AI headlines this week.

We know the AI trade has been getting smoked recently, but when you dig into the numbers, the carnage is pretty remarkable. AI infrastructure names have been getting hit the hardest. Here are some scary 3-month performance numbers: CRWV down -52%, SMCI down -37%, VRT down -35%, and ORCL down -20%. Semiconductor stocks have also cratered from their 52-week highs: NVDA down -36%, MU down -42%, AVGO down -32%, and TSM down -23%.

The tech money has been rotating into defensive sectors. And the most defensive tech stock of them all, Apple, has massively outperformed as traders migrate to the perceived "safe-haven" tech trade (see charts below). For context, AAPL is +15% over the last three months, and is only 2% from its all-time high. Quite a contrast.

We'll find out shortly if Nvidia's earnings help AI stocks find their footing.
