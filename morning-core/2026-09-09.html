September 9, 2026

Oil Hits $100,
Tech Safe Haven?

Brent crude hits $100/bbl. Tech holding up — investors hiding in stable AI-driven earnings. Nick Colas: S&P valuation problem — multiples shrinking despite strong earnings; anything above +7-8% returns needs lower oil or AI CapEx clarity. Citi Earnings Revisions Index positive 21 straight weeks. Stocks outperformed bonds 28% annualized over 76 months — biggest gap in 100 years.

Nicholas Lampone
Chief Strategy Officer & Investment Advisor · Pine Valley Investments

U.S. Futures & World Markets

Stocks are lower premarket as investors go a little more risk-off with Brent crude hitting $100 per barrel. Headlines out of Iran did little to ease concerns that oil prices are headed lower anytime soon.

Tech stocks have held up fairly well, as AI demand continues to help the group outperform. With so many uncertainties floating around, it's almost as if the rotation into tech stocks is the "safe haven" trade. If other parts of the economy remain vulnerable to higher oil prices and impending rate hikes, why not hide out in names with stable and growing earnings?

Nick Colas of DataTrek Research on current valuations: the S&P 500 has a valuation problem. Earnings have been strong all year, but the index's multiple continues to shrink. On the plus side, reasonable expected 1-year returns (+7–8%) no longer require anything other than stable multiples and inline earnings reports. Anything more likely needs lower oil prices and/or clarity on AI CapEx returns.

Speculative positioning in gold has reversed sharply. Goldman still sees prices reaching $4,900/oz by year-end. Goldman via Daily Chartbook.
